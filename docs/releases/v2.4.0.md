## Summary

Working prewarmed track handoffs, consistent loudness and a bounded playback resolver.

## Changes

- Activated prewarm scheduling and preserved matching warm streams on skip.
- Passed measured loudness profiles through to FFmpeg for both warm and cold starts.
- Added a 90-second resolution watchdog and visible buffering state.
- Raised the default Opus bitrate to 128 kbps and expanded parallel music search.
- Fixed audio-URL retry races, persistent caching, fresh-install setup mode and OAuth response handling.

## Upgrade

Existing explicit bitrate settings take precedence over the new default. Review `RHAPSOD_OPUS_BITRATE` if adopting 128 kbps. Rebuild, wait for idle playback and restart; check the setup wizard on new installations.

## Verification

Queue two tracks and inspect the handoff and loudness. Confirm buffering appears during resolution and a failed track does not prevent the queue from continuing.
