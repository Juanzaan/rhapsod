## Summary

Playback and reconnect reliability improvements for low-resource hosts.

## Changes

- Corrected the TeamSpeak Opus Music wire codec to 5.
- Reset failed connections before retrying, added heartbeat tolerance and bounded reconnect backoff.
- Preserved queued tracks across graceful restarts and cancelled playlist expansion after stop or clear.
- Added UID-based removal permissions and HTTPS/private-address checks for direct audio URLs.
- Reduced FFmpeg probing, prefetched three tracks and moved persistence off the synchronous playback path.

## Upgrade

Back up configuration and data. Review direct audio links: `http:` inputs are rejected; use public `https:` streams. Rebuild with the lockfile belonging to this tag.

## Verification

Queue several tracks, skip between them and restart while idle. Confirm the saved queue restores for connected requesters and private network URLs are rejected.
